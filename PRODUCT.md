# Octave CRM AI Product Context

Octave CRM AI is a branded CRM and workflow platform for teams that need customer data, relationship management, automation, and AI assistance in one deployable workspace.

Primary audience:

- Sales, RevOps, and operations teams managing customer records and activity
- Implementation teams configuring CRM objects, views, and workflows
- Business owners who want a self-hostable CRM surface under the Octave brand

Core promise:

Octave CRM AI helps teams manage customer relationships, automate routine work, and keep CRM data actionable without giving up deployment control.
