# Octave CRM AI Design Notes

Octave CRM AI should feel operational, precise, and trustworthy. The UI is a working CRM surface, so favor clarity, dense but calm information layouts, and restrained visual styling.

Brand cues:

- Product name: Octave CRM AI
- Company: Octave Consultancy and Technology Services
- Primary visual asset: the Octave line-wave mark in `packages/twenty-front/public/images/brand/`
- Tone: professional, direct, implementation-ready

Avoid marketing-heavy layout patterns inside the application shell. The product should open directly into useful CRM workflows.
